\section{Qualitative Case-to-Theme Mapping}
\label{sec:qualitative-mapping}
The fixed purposive sample contains 40 cases from 40 tasks. The original selection used an existing candidate list and prior review-status coverage; it was not a fresh random draw from all source-exposed failures. The original 30-case development and 10-case rule-check history is retained in the replication records. Themes are descriptive, nonexclusive organizing concepts, not estimates of benchmark prevalence. The remaining cases are retained as other or case-specific observations; this bucket is not a shared mechanism. The mapping below is an evidence synthesis of the reviewed cases, not an additional independent human annotation round.
