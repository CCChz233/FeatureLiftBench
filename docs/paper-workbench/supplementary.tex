\section{Token Expenditure by Final Outcome}
\paragraph{Token expenditure varies across outcomes.}
Table~\ref{tab:execution-effort} reports token distributions from 764 runs
with recoverable complete ledgers. Among these records, failed runs have higher
median expenditure than successful runs for Pro, Flash, and OSS; the direction
is reversed for Luna, GLM, and Qwen. For example, Pro's medians are 2.24 million
tokens for successes and 2.71 million for failures, whereas Qwen's are 2.13
million and 1.30 million. Thus, higher expenditure does not consistently
coincide with a successful outcome. These are descriptive comparisons of
different task sets. Coverage also varies: GLM's failure summary includes only
10 of 82 failures, so it does not characterize all of that configuration's
failed runs.

\section{Successful-Artifact Footprints and Sensitivity}
The adjusted analysis includes 485 successful artifacts from 115 tasks with at
least two successful configurations, spanning 97 repositories. Seven singleton
successes are excluded. The sample contains 52 Direct, 55 Adapted, and eight
Composite tasks. Table~\ref{tab:matched-footprint} gives the estimates plotted in
Figure 8 of the main manuscript. RRES denotes implementation size relative to
the feasible reference package; Copy denotes detected textual source overlap.
The task-adjusted estimand, symmetric configuration-effect center, and bootstrap
procedure are defined in the main manuscript's analysis protocol. Intervals are
pointwise 95\% task-bootstrap intervals, not simultaneous pairwise comparisons.

\paragraph{Sensitivity to weighting and repository dependence.}
Giving each task total weight one preserves the direction of every
configuration's point estimate on both measures. Relative changes in the
estimated RRES ratios are at most 4.71\%, and changes in Copy are at most
0.78 pp. The RRES ordering of Luna and OSS reverses under this weighting,
so their near-equal point estimates do not support an ordering between them.
Repository-cluster bootstrap intervals give the same qualitative relation to
the reference centers as the main intervals, including Qwen's Copy interval
spanning zero. These checks support the footprint profiles, not a complete
pairwise ranking; overlap of two displayed intervals is not a test of their
pairwise difference.


These measurements characterize successful artifacts only. They do not measure
minimality, maintainability, semantic superiority, or deliberate reuse strategy,
and do not extrapolate to tasks on which a configuration fails.
